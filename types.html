Types of Recyclable Materials

Different materials are recycled in different ways. Here are the four main types.

Plastic

Plastic bottles and containers can be melted and turned into new bottles, clothes and furniture.

Tip: Empty and rinse plastic before recycling.

Paper

Newspapers, boxes and office paper are soaked, cleaned and pressed into new paper.

Tip: Wet or greasy paper (like pizza boxes) cannot be recycled.

Metal

Aluminium and steel cans can be recycled again and again without losing quality.

Tip: Crush cans to save space in the bin.

Electronic Waste

Old phones, laptops and batteries contain valuable metals and harmful chemicals.

Tip: Take e-waste to a special collection point.
