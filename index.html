Why Is Recycling Important?

Saves Resources

Recycling reduces the need to cut trees, mine metals and drill for oil.

Saves Energy

Making products from recycled materials usually uses less energy than making them from new materials.

Reduces Waste

Less rubbish goes to landfills, which keeps our land, water and air cleaner.

Easy Tips to Start Today

  • Rinse bottles and cans before recycling them.
  • Keep paper dry and clean.
  • Never put batteries or phones in normal trash.
  • Carry a reusable bag and water bottle.
